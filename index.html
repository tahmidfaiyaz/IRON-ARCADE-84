HOW TO PLAY

You have 30 seconds to put in the work. Press SPACE or tap LIFT! for a rep.

Let the bar recover, then hit the cyan zone — 0.7–1.05 seconds after your last rep — for a perfect lift. Chain perfect reps to build a 5× combo.

  • Good rep: 100 points + 10 XP.
  • Perfect rep: 150 points × combo + 20 XP.
  • The bar needs 0.45 seconds to recover. Holding a key won’t repeat lifts.
  • Finish a round to bank your XP and best score. Restarting discards the current round.

P / ESC pauses. R restarts. Leaving the tab pauses your workout automatically.
